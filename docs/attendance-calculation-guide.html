1. Configurable Work Schedule

The dashboard uses the shifts entered in the Parameters tab. For AGROSATI, enter these shift starts:

All salary calculations use 22 payable days and 8 hours per shift. These values remain editable in Parameters.

2. H-: Late Arrival and Early Departure

The entry tolerance is 15 minutes. After that tolerance, H- is deducted in progressive 15-minute blocks.

Arrival after scheduled startH- deduction
0 to 15 minutes0 minutes
16 to 30 minutes15 minutes (0.25 h)
31 to 45 minutes30 minutes (0.50 h)
46 to 60 minutes45 minutes (0.75 h)
Arrival H- = ceil((late minutes - entry tolerance) / 15) x 0.25 hour

Early departure follows the same 15-minute blocks after the editable exit tolerance. With a shift ending at 16:00 and a 10-minute exit tolerance:

Actual exitH- deduction
15:50 or later0 minutes
15:49 to 15:3515 minutes (0.25 h)
15:34 to 15:2030 minutes (0.50 h)

3. Absence Deduction

One confirmed absence day

For exactly one confirmed absence, the dashboard converts the day into payable hours without rounding it to a full day.

Converted day = 1 x 22 / 30 = 0.7333 day
Absence hours = 0.7333 x 8 = 5.8667 hours (about 5 h 52 min)
One-day absence deduction = absence hours x hourly salary

Two or more confirmed absence days

The previous rule remains active: convert days with days x 22 / 30, then round to the nearest full payable day.

Confirmed absencesConversionPayable absence days used for H-
2 days2 x 22 / 30 = 1.46671 day
3 days3 x 22 / 30 = 2.202 days
4 days4 x 22 / 30 = 2.93333 days

4. Salary Values

Daily salary = base salary / 22
Hourly salary = base salary / 22 / 8

The full H- amount combines the absence deduction and machine-based lateness/early-departure deduction.

H- amount = absence deduction + (late/early H- hours x hourly salary)

5. Worked Example

Employee base salary: 24,000 DA

One confirmed absence: 0.7333 day = 5.8667 hours

Additional late/early time: 1.5 hours

H+ declared in payroll: 1,500 DA

Daily salary = 24,000 / 22 = 1,090.91 DA

Hourly salary = 24,000 / 22 / 8 = 136.36 DA

Absence deduction = 5.8667 x 136.36 = 800.00 DA

Late/early deduction = 1.5 x 136.36 = 204.55 DA

Total H- = 800.00 + 204.55 = 1,004.55 DA

Final balance = 24,000 + 1,500 - 1,004.55 = 24,495.45 DA

6. H+ Overtime

Current dashboard behavior: the displayed H+ amount is read from the legal payroll journal. It is not yet calculated from machine exit time.

The agreed future machine rule for H+ is:

  • Any overtime from 1 to 15 minutes is credited as 15 minutes.
  • After the first 15 minutes, each started 5 minutes adds 5 minutes.
  • Example: a 16:00 scheduled exit and 17:00 actual exit equals 60 minutes H+.

Machine H+ should be compared with H+ declared in payroll. Only approved/payroll-declared H+ should be added to the final salary balance.

7. Final Salary Balance Display

Final balance = salary base + real H+ from payroll - calculated H-

The dashboard intentionally does not display Net a payer in this control. It focuses on the attendance effect on the salary base.
